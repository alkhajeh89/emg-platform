# AI Experience Guidelines

## Purpose
Defines the visual language for AI agent interaction.

## Scope
All AI-powered features.

## Business Context
Module 9 (AI Orchestration).

## Principles
- **Transparency:** Clear AI status indicators.
- **Explainability:** Mandatory citation presentation.
- **Human-Centric:** AI supports, doesn't mandate.

## Enterprise Guidelines
- AI output vs. human-generated content styling.

## Accessibility
- AI outputs must be fully screen-reader compatible.

## Internationalization
- Language-agnostic confidence display.

## Usage Guidelines
- AI interaction patterns.

## Anti-Patterns
- Presenting AI output as absolute truth.
