# Design Principles

## Purpose
Establishes the core philosophical directives for all EMG™ frontend interfaces, ensuring a cohesive and trustworthy user experience.

## Scope
Applicable to all frontend surfaces and design system components.

## Design Philosophy
The design must reflect the platform's commitment to explainability, trust, and human-centered design, serving as an extension of the [Enterprise AI Vision](../../product/EMG_PRODUCT_VISION.md).

## Business Context
Adheres to [ADR-014](../../architecture/EMG_ADR-014_Enterprise_Presentation_Architecture.md).

## Principles
- **Consistency:** Uniform visual language across all surfaces.
- **Explainability:** AI outputs must be grounded, cited, and transparent.
- **Trust:** Visual cues must communicate system state and uncertainty clearly.
- **Enterprise Usability:** Designed for complex, high-stakes decision-making.
- **Human-centered AI:** AI augments, does not replace, human agency.
- **Accessibility-first:** Inclusive design for all users.
