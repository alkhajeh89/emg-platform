# Executive Summary

**Classification:** L1 Executive Authority
**Status:** Ratified and Effective
**Approving authority:** Office of the CTO
**Effective date:** 2026-08-01
**Authority scope:** Executive vision and strategic direction only

This document is the canonical, ratified L1 Executive Authority for the EMG
Platform. EMG's executive direction is to strengthen organizational resilience
and accountable decision-making by preserving institutional knowledge,
connecting decisions to evidence and provenance, and keeping human authority
over high-consequence outcomes.

The platform is intended to provide leaders and authorized practitioners with
explainable, traceable decision support grounded in governed organizational
memory. Its strategic outcomes are improved continuity, clearer accountability,
and greater confidence in the evidence behind decisions.

## Authority Boundaries

- Product scope, product boundaries, editions, and release definitions are
  governed exclusively by
  `docs/product/EMG_PRODUCT_ARCHITECTURE_FREEZE.md`.
- Technical architecture is governed exclusively by accepted Architecture
  Decision Records (ADRs) within their stated scope.
- This Executive Summary does not amend the Product Architecture Freeze,
  create architecture, or convert supporting executive material into binding
  authority.
- Delivery status and implementation evidence remain L4 records and must be
  verified against the repository and the applicable delivery documentation.
