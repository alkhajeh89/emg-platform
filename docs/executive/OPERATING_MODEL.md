# Operating Model

**Classification:** Supporting executive operating-model narrative;
non-governing unless a decision is separately ratified

The platform is intended to operate through clear ownership, governed change,
and evidence-based delivery:

- **Ownership Registry (ADR-016):** The accepted architecture assigns
  accountable owners and operational stewards to platform components. This
  document does not alter those assignments.
- **Governed Change Management:** Architecture changes follow the accepted ADR
  process. Product-scope changes follow the Product Architecture Freeze's
  control process.
- **Documentation Governance (GR-001):**
  `docs/governance/GR-001_EMG_DOCUMENTATION_GOVERNANCE_FRAMEWORK.md`, ratified
  jointly by the Office of the CTO and the Architecture Board and effective
  2026-08-01, governs documentation authority and conflict precedence across
  the repository. It does not redefine product scope, architecture, or this
  operating model.
- **Sprint-Based Execution:** Delivery is managed through sprint and phase
  records. Those L4 records report implementation progress but do not define
  product scope or architecture.
