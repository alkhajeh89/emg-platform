# Operating Model

The platform is designed to operate under a declared board-governed model:
- **Ownership Registry (ADR-016)**: The registry is declared as the mechanism to assign an accountable owner and operational steward to every module, service, and infrastructure component.
- **Governed Change Management**: Architectural changes are proposed via an Architecture Board (ADR registry), intended to ensure adherence to the frozen platform baseline.
- **Sprint-Based Execution**: Continuous, phased delivery of capabilities is managed within defined sprint cycles, intended to ensure iterative quality and velocity.
