# Executive Glossary

- **Decision Intelligence**: Technology field focused on augmenting decision-making by correlating data, evidence, and human oversight.
- **Evidence**: Verified data or facts used to support organizational information.
- **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)**: Design principle requiring human verification for high-consequence system actions.
- **Knowledge Graph**: A structured representation of organizational entities and their relationships.
- **Provenance**: The documented origin and history of a piece of data.
- **Trust-Scoring**: Quantitative evaluation mechanism for the reliability and veracity of information.
- **Ontology**: A formal definition of common terms, concepts, and relationships shared across the organization.
- **Grounded AI**: Artificial intelligence design principle basing output on verifiable organizational evidence.
