# Problem Statement

Organizations facing high-stakes operational environments suffer from:
1. **Decision Ambiguity**: Critical decisions lack traceable evidence and clear provenance.
2. **Knowledge Fragility**: Institutional knowledge is trapped in silos or lost through turnover.
3. **Operational Blind Spots**: Decision-makers cannot effectively correlate disparate data to understand organizational risk.
4. **Governance Gaps**: Automated decision-support tools often function as "black boxes" without sufficient oversight, trust-scoring, or human verification.
