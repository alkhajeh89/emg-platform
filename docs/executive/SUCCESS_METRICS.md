# Success Metrics

The following metrics are proposed for future measurement:
- **Decision Speed & Efficiency**: Reduction in time-to-decision via streamlined evidence gathering.
- **Decision Quality**: Improvement in trust-scores and reduction in manual rework.
- **Traceability**: Percentage of automated decisions with complete, cited provenance records.
- **Approval Throughput**: Effectiveness of human oversight gateways for high-consequence decisions.
