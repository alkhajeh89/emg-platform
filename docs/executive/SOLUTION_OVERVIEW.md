# Solution Overview

**Classification:** Supporting executive narrative; non-governing
**Authority:** Subordinate to the ratified `EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md`, the Product
Architecture Freeze for product scope, and accepted ADRs for architecture

The EMG Platform is intended to turn governed organizational knowledge into
accountable decision support. The following are **non-governing solution
characteristics**, not alternative product pillars and not independent product
or architecture definitions:

1. **Governed Knowledge Foundation**: Common concepts, evidence, provenance,
   and controls support consistent interpretation of organizational knowledge.
2. **Explainable Intelligence**: Grounded, cited, and trust-scored capabilities
   are intended to explain the evidence behind decision support as their
   runtime integrations are delivered.
3. **Accountable Oversight**: Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) controls are intended to
   preserve human authority over high-consequence decision points as approved
   enforcement capabilities are implemented.

Auditability by construction is an executive objective. Implementation status
must be established from repository evidence and must not be inferred from this
overview.
