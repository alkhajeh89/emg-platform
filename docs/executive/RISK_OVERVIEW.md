# Risk Overview

### Evidenced Controls
- **Audit-First Design**: The audit platform implements event-based provenance tracking (Postgres).
- **Human Authority**: HITL approval gateways are a documented design principle (Architecture Baseline v1.0).

### Unresolved Risks
- **AI Explainability**: Trust scoring and citation mechanisms are planned capabilities, not yet operational.
- **Storage Independence**: The decoupling of persistence from business logic is an architectural design, not yet fully operationalized across all modules.
