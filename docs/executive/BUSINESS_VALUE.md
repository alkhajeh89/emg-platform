# Business Value

Investing in the EMG Platform is intended to drive:
- **Operational Trust**: Decisions are designed to be verifiable, intended to reduce risk and ambiguity.
- **Enhanced Decision Quality**: The platform aims to provide leaders with clear, cited, and scored insights.
- **Efficient Governance**: Automated policy enforcement and clear ownership (ADR-016) are intended to reduce manual overhead and compliance risk.
- **Knowledge Retention**: Captured provenance and organizational context aim to ensure institutional intelligence is preserved.
