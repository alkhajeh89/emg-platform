The problem

Enterprise knowledge is fragmented. AI amplifies the cost.

Critical context lives across applications, databases, documents and people. Conventional retrieval can find information, but enterprises also need to know what changed, why it changed, who was authorized, and what evidence supports a decision.

01

Persistent organizational memory

Represent entities, relationships, decisions and revisions as durable enterprise memory rather than disposable prompt context.

02

Governed by design

Authorization, auditability, provenance and controlled mutation are architectural concerns—not features added after deployment.

03

AI-ready context

Expose structured, explainable context for AI-assisted workflows while maintaining enterprise control over authoritative state.

Platform architecture

Built as infrastructure, not another chatbot.

EMG separates authoritative persistence, graph projection, decision access, authorization and audit so enterprises can adopt AI without collapsing governance boundaries.
